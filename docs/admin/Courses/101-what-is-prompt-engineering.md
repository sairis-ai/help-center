## What is a Prompt for AI?   
A **prompt** is a structured input or instruction given to a Large Language Model (LLM) to guide its response, shaping the output by providing context, constraints, or specific directions. In AI interactions, prompts can take many forms, from simple questions and keywords to detailed instructions, code snippets, or creative writing samples. The way you craft a prompt directly impacts the quality, accuracy, and relevance of the AI's response, making **effective prompt design essential to unlocking the full potential of AI-powered interactions.**   
 
 
## What is Prompt Engineering?   
**Prompt Engineering** is both an art and a science-an essential skill for shaping AI interactions to produce meaningful, relevant, and high-quality outputs. It involves designing and optimizing prompts that provide AI models with clear instructions, context, and examples, guiding them toward the desired response.   
 
Think of prompt engineering as **giving the AI a roadmap**, ensuring it understands your intent and follows a structured path to generate accurate and useful results. In Sairis, mastering prompt engineering empowers users to refine AI-driven outputs, aligning them with corporate knowledge, brand standards, and business objectives-ultimately transforming AI into a **powerful extension of your workforce.**   
 
 
## Types of Prompts in Sairis   
 
Understanding how different types of prompts function in Sairis allows you to control AI behavior effectively. Each prompt type plays a role in shaping interactions, from system-wide configurations to specific user commands.   
 
### **System Prompts**   
System prompts define the **foundation of AI behavior**, ensuring security, compliance, and platform-specific configurations. These prompts are managed by Sairis and **are not user-configurable.**   
 
- Establishes core AI behavior   
- Manages AI safety and compliance guardrails   
- Represents Sairis platform's secure communication with the AI model   
- Not exposed to customers; managed exclusively between Sairis and model manufacturers   
 
> **Note:** System prompts are managed internally and are not the focus of this prompt engineering guide.   
 
### **Instructions (Metaprompts)**   
Instructions define how AI behaves within specific business contexts. They are the **primary mechanism for shaping AI behavior** in Sairis and can be customized based on user needs.   
 
- Guide AI behavior for different use cases   
- Can be broad (personality-based) or specific (task-driven)   
- Commonly used for:   
  - General conversation behaviors   
  - Industry-specific interactions   
  - Task-based responses   
- Managed by users with appropriate permissions   
- Can be applied per conversation or templatized in **Blueprints** for consistency   
 
> **Pro Tip:** If you find yourself using the AI for a task more than two or three times, save it as a **Blueprint**-this ensures consistency and saves time every time you need it!   
 
### **User Input Prompts**   
User input prompts are the **direct interactions between users and AI** during a conversation. These prompts trigger AI responses based on instructions and system settings.   
 
- Function as real-time commands or questions   
- Typically **single-turn inputs**   
- Can be saved as **Conversation Starters** in Blueprints for repeated use   
 
 
## How Prompts Work Together   
 
Prompts in Sairis function in **layers**, with each type serving a specific role in shaping AI behavior and responses.   
 
1. **System prompts** - Ensure safe, compliant AI operations at the platform level.   
2. **Instructions** - Shape AI behavior to align with business needs and user preferences.   
3. **User input prompts** - Trigger AI responses within the framework established by system prompts and instructions.   
 
By understanding how these elements interact, you can create AI workflows that **generate predictable, high-quality results.**   
 
 
## Key Terminology   
 
| **Term** | **Definition** | **Usage** | 
|:-----|:-----------|:------| 
| **Prompts** | The input or instruction given to an LLM | Creates effective, reliable AI interactions | 
| **Prompt Engineering** | The practice of designing and optimizing AI instructions | Shapes AI behavior for specific tasks | 
| **Blueprint** | A template that saves Instructions and Conversation Starters | Enables consistent reuse of effective prompts | 
| **Instructions** | Customizable metaprompts that guide AI behavior | Defines response format, rules, and context | 
| **Conversation Starter** | A saved user input prompt | Creates standardized conversation openings | 
 
 
## Examples in Practice   
 
### **Example 1: User Inputs**   
A well-crafted **user input prompt** leads to **more accurate and structured AI responses.**   
 
> John needs to draft a project status update. Instead of writing:   
> ``` 
> "Give me a status update." 
> ``` 
> He writes:   
> ``` 
> "Write a brief status update for my project highlighting three key achievements from this week and two challenges we need to address." 
> ``` 
> The AI responds with a clear, professional update based on this specific user input.   
 
### **Example 2: Instructions and User Input Working Together**   
By combining **Instructions** with user input prompts, AI can produce **consistent, structured responses** that follow predefined guidelines.   
 
> Jane frequently needs to analyze customer feedback and generate action items. In her Sairis settings panel, she creates **Instructions** that specify:   
> - Always categorize feedback into themes   
> - Present findings in a structured format with bullet points   
> - Include specific recommendations   
> - Prioritize action items by impact and effort   
 
> Now when Jane inputs:   
> ``` 
> "Review this customer feedback and suggest next steps." 
> ``` 
> The AI follows her predetermined format and approach. Satisfied with the results, Jane saves these **Instructions into a Blueprint called "Customer Feedback Analysis."**   
 
> With appropriate permissions, Jane shares this Blueprint with her team, allowing them to generate similarly structured analyses from their own customer feedback.


# End User: Quick Start

So your an End User of Sairis.

As an End User, you are the primary consumer of the AI assistant platform, empowered to leverage powerful tools for productivity and creativity. Your role is centered on utilizing the system to ask questions, generate ideas, summarize documents, and interact with assigned content. You'll have the flexibility to control AI instructions, select different AI models, and use various tools like web and news searches. 

The upcoming videos will demonstrate how you can interact with the Sairis AI Assistant, Launchpad, Knowledge Bases, Blueprints and Content.  How to switch between AI modes, showcasing the versatile ways you can maximize your experience as an End User. 

During the videos you'll discover how to effectively navigate and utilize the platform's user-focused features.

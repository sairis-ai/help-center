# How to Write Great Prompts - Part 2: Intermediate

## Building on Basics

Now that you've mastered the fundamentals from Part 1, it's time to explore more sophisticated prompting techniques. These methods will help you get even better results and handle more complex tasks with AI.

## Role-Based Prompting

Instead of just asking AI to help you, assign it a specific role or expertise. This helps the AI respond from a particular perspective and knowledge base.

### How It Works:
Start your prompt with "As a [role/expert]..." or "You are a [role/expert]..."

### Examples:

**Basic prompt**: "How do I improve team productivity?"

**Role-based prompt**: "As an experienced team manager, what are the top 5 strategies you'd recommend for improving team productivity without increasing workload stress?"

**Another example**: "You are a customer service expert. Help me write a response to an angry customer who received the wrong order. The response should acknowledge the mistake, fix the problem, and try to retain their business."

### Useful Roles:
- **Project manager**: For planning and organization questions
- **HR specialist**: For people and workplace issues  
- **Financial advisor**: For budgeting and cost decisions
- **Marketing expert**: For communication and promotion questions
- **Experienced consultant**: For strategic business advice

## Using Examples

Examples are one of the most powerful tools in prompting. They show AI exactly what you want instead of just telling it.

### Show, Don't Tell

**Instead of**: "Write in a professional tone"
**Try**: "Write in a professional tone like this example: 'Thank you for bringing this matter to our attention. We've reviewed the situation and have identified steps to address your concerns promptly.'"

### Example Strategies:

#### Good Example:
"Write a project status email similar to this style: 'Hi team, Quick update on the Johnson project. We're 75% complete and on track for Friday's deadline. Sarah finished the design phase yesterday, and Tom starts development testing tomorrow. One heads up: we might need an extra day for client feedback review. I'll keep you posted. Thanks for the great work so far!'"

#### What NOT to Do:
"Write a meeting reminder email. Don't make it sound demanding like 'You must attend this meeting.' Instead, make it sound collaborative and informative."

#### Before/After:
"Improve this email to sound more professional:
BEFORE: 'Hey, can you get me those numbers? Need them ASAP.'
AFTER: 'Hi Sarah, when you have a moment, could you please send me the Q3 sales figures? I'm working on the board presentation and would appreciate having them by end of day Thursday. Thanks!'"

## Step-by-Step Thinking

For complex problems, ask AI to work through the problem step-by-step. This leads to better reasoning and more thorough solutions.

### How to Ask for It:

**Add phrases like:**
- "Walk me through your thinking step-by-step"
- "Let's break this down into steps"
- "Think through this systematically"
- "Show your reasoning for each recommendation"

### Example:
**Instead of**: "Should I hire a new team member or use freelancers?"

**Step-by-step version**: "I'm trying to decide whether to hire a full-time team member or use freelancers for our growing workload. Walk me through the decision-making process step-by-step. Consider factors like cost, quality control, time management, and long-term planning. Show your reasoning for each factor."

### Complex Problem Template:
"I need to solve [problem]. Let's approach this systematically:
1. First, help me identify the root causes
2. Then, brainstorm possible solutions for each cause
3. Finally, evaluate the pros and cons of the top 3 solutions
Let's start with step 1."

## Using Constraints

Set clear boundaries and requirements to get more focused, useful responses.

### Types of Constraints:

#### Time Constraints:
- "I have 15 minutes to prepare for this call..."
- "This needs to be implemented by month-end..."
- "Give me something I can do in the next hour..."

#### Resource Constraints:
- "We have a budget of $5,000..."
- "I'm working with a team of 3 people..."
- "Using only free tools..."

#### Audience Constraints:
- "This is for people who aren't tech-savvy..."
- "The CEO will be reading this..."
- "Designed for new employees..."

### Example:
"Create a team-building activity plan with these constraints: 2-hour time limit, budget under $200, for a remote team of 12 people, must work across different time zones, and should focus on improving communication. Give me 3 different options with pros and cons for each."

## Multi-Part Prompting

Break complex requests into smaller, connected parts for better results.

### Sequential Prompting:
Instead of asking for everything at once, build up your request:

**Step 1**: "Help me understand the key factors I should consider when choosing between different project management tools for my team."

**Step 2**: "Based on those factors, here are my specific needs: [list your requirements]. Which factors should I prioritize?"

**Step 3**: "Now help me create evaluation criteria and a scoring system to compare 3 different tools."

### Iterative Improvement:
Start with a basic request, then refine:

**Round 1**: "Draft an agenda for our monthly team meeting."

**Round 2**: "Good start. Now add time estimates for each item and include a section for celebrating team wins."

**Round 3**: "Perfect. Now adapt this for a virtual meeting format with engagement techniques."

## Multiple Perspectives

Ask AI to consider multiple viewpoints to get more balanced, comprehensive responses.

### Stakeholder Views:
"Analyze this decision from three perspectives:
1. From my perspective as the manager (focusing on team productivity)
2. From the team's perspective (focusing on workload and satisfaction)  
3. From the company's perspective (focusing on costs and results)"

### Pro/Con Analysis:
"I'm considering implementing a 4-day work week. Give me arguments both FOR and AGAINST this decision. For each argument, explain the reasoning and provide a real example."

### Different Scenarios:
"Help me plan for three scenarios:
- Best case: Everything goes perfectly
- Worst case: Major problems arise
- Most likely: Some challenges but overall success
For each scenario, what should I prepare for?"

## Templates and Frameworks

Ask AI to create reusable templates and frameworks you can use repeatedly.

### Examples:

**Meeting Template**:
"Create a meeting agenda template I can use for weekly team meetings. Include sections for updates, problem-solving, planning, and team building. Make it flexible enough to work for teams of 5-15 people."

**Communication Framework**:
"Design a framework for giving constructive feedback to team members. It should have 3-4 steps that help me organize my thoughts and deliver feedback in a supportive way."

**Decision-Making Tool**:
"Create a simple decision-making template I can use when choosing between different options. Include criteria for evaluation and a scoring system."

## Refinement

Learn to build on AI responses to get exactly what you need.

### Refinement Phrases:
- "That's good, but can you make it more..."
- "I like the structure, but can you adjust the tone to be..."
- "Perfect format, now customize it for..."
- "Great start, but I need more detail on..."

### Example:
**First prompt**: "Help me write a job description for a marketing coordinator."

**Response**: [AI provides basic job description]

**Refinement**: "This is a good foundation. Now make it more appealing to recent graduates, emphasize our collaborative culture, and add a section about growth opportunities."

**Further refinement**: "Excellent. One more adjustment - can you make the requirements section less intimidating while still maintaining our standards?"

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Over-Complicated Roles
**Don't**: "You are a senior executive consultant with 20 years of experience in Fortune 500 companies specializing in organizational transformation..."
**Do**: "As an experienced business consultant..."

### Mistake 2: Too Many Examples
**Don't**: Provide 5 different examples in one prompt
**Do**: Give 1-2 clear, relevant examples

### Mistake 3: Forgetting Basics
Even with advanced techniques, still include:
- Clear context
- Specific format requirements  
- Your goals and constraints

## Combining Techniques

The real power comes from combining multiple techniques in one prompt:

**Example combining role-based + examples + constraints**:
"As an HR specialist, help me create a performance review template. Use a collaborative tone like this example: 'Let's discuss your achievements this quarter and explore opportunities for growth.' The template should work for 30-minute meetings, include sections for both accomplishments and development areas, and end with goal-setting for the next quarter."

## Key Takeaways

1. **Role assignment** helps AI respond from specific expertise
2. **Examples** are more powerful than descriptions
3. **Step-by-step thinking** improves reasoning quality
4. **Constraints** focus responses and make them more practical
5. **Multi-part prompting** handles complex requests better
6. **Multiple perspectives** provide more balanced solutions
7. **Templates and frameworks** create reusable tools
8. **Iteration and refinement** help you get exactly what you need

Practice these techniques with your regular tasks, and you'll notice a significant improvement in the quality and usefulness of AI responses.

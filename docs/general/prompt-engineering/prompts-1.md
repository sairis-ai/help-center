# How to Write Great Prompts - Part 1: Basics

Master the essentials of communicating with AI

## Why This Matters

The difference between a mediocre AI response and a brilliant one often comes down to how you ask the question. Think of prompting like giving directions to a very capable assistant who doesn't know your context, preferences, or goals. The clearer and more specific your instructions, the better the results you'll get.

**What you'll gain from better prompting:**
- Get useful answers on the first try instead of going back and forth
- Save time by avoiding vague or irrelevant responses  
- Receive outputs that match your style and needs
- Unlock AI's full potential as a thinking partner

## The Golden Rule

**Be specific about what you want, why you want it, and how you want it delivered.**

This simple principle transforms weak prompts into powerful ones.

## Principle 1: Be Clear

Don't worry about being too wordy if it means being clearer. AI can handle detailed instructions better than vague ones.

### Bad Example:
"Help with my presentation."

### Good Example:
"Help me create an outline for a 10-minute presentation to my team about our Q3 sales results. I want to highlight our wins, address the challenges we faced, and outline our strategy for Q4. The tone should be honest but optimistic."

**Why it works:** The good example tells the AI exactly what type of help you need, who the audience is, what to include, how long it should be, and what tone to use.

## Principle 2: Give Context

Context is the background information that helps AI understand your situation and give relevant advice.

### What to Include:
- **Your role**: Are you a manager, team member, business owner?
- **Your audience**: Who will see or use this output?
- **Your constraints**: Time limits, budget, company policies, etc.
- **Your goals**: What are you trying to achieve?

### Without Context:
"Write an email about the meeting."

### With Context:
"I'm a project manager and need to write an email to my team of 8 people postponing tomorrow's weekly status meeting. The reason is that our client moved up their deadline, so we need to focus on deliverables today. I want to reschedule for Friday and keep the tone professional but understanding of the inconvenience."

## Principle 3: Specify Format

Tell AI exactly how you want the information presented. This saves time and gives you something you can use immediately.

### Format Options:
- **Length**: "In 3 bullet points," "500 words," "one paragraph"
- **Structure**: "As a step-by-step guide," "in a table," "as a checklist"
- **Style**: "Professional tone," "casual and friendly," "formal business language"

### Example:
"Explain the pros and cons of remote work policies. Format as a simple table with two columns - 'Advantages' and 'Disadvantages' - with 5 points in each column. Write in plain language that any employee could understand."

## The WHAT-WHY-HOW Framework

Use this simple framework to structure your prompts:

### WHAT
What specific task or question do you have?

### WHY  
Why do you need this? What's the context or goal?

### HOW
How do you want the response formatted and delivered?

### Example:
**WHAT**: "Create talking points for a difficult conversation with an underperforming team member"

**WHY**: "I'm a new manager and this is my first time having a performance discussion. I want to be direct but supportive, and focus on specific behaviors rather than personality"

**HOW**: "Give me 5-7 key points I can reference during the conversation, written as brief phrases I can glance at. Include one opening statement and one way to end on a positive note"

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Being Vague
**Instead of**: "Make this better"
**Try**: "Improve this email to sound more professional while keeping it friendly. The recipient is a potential client I've never met."

### Mistake 2: No Context
**Instead of**: "How do I handle this situation?"
**Try**: "I'm a team lead and one of my team members consistently misses deadlines, affecting the whole project. How should I address this in our one-on-one meeting this week?"

### Mistake 3: Assuming AI Knows You
**Instead of**: "Write a job posting"
**Try**: "Write a job posting for a marketing coordinator at our 50-person software company. We want someone with 2-3 years experience who can handle both digital marketing and events. Keep the tone professional but not stuffy - we're a pretty casual workplace."

### Mistake 4: Multiple Requests
**Instead of**: "Help me plan a team meeting, write follow-up emails, and create a project timeline"
**Try**: Break this into separate, focused requests for better results

## Useful Templates

### Problem-Solving:
"I'm facing [specific situation]. My goal is [what you want to achieve]. My constraints are [limitations you're working with]. What are 3 different approaches I could take?"

### Communication:
"Help me write a [type of communication] to [audience] about [topic]. The tone should be [desired tone] and the length should be [target length]. Key points to include: [list your main points]."

### Decision-Making:
"I need to decide between [option A] and [option B] for [situation]. Here are the key factors I'm considering: [list factors]. Help me think through the pros and cons of each option."

### Planning:
"I need to create a plan for [project/goal] that needs to be completed by [deadline]. I have [resources/constraints]. Break this down into main phases with key milestones."

## Testing Your Prompts

### Good responses are:
- **Relevant**: Directly addresses what you asked for
- **Actionable**: You can immediately use or implement the advice
- **Appropriate**: Matches the tone and style you requested
- **Complete**: Covers all the points you mentioned

### Signs to improve:
- The response is too generic or broad
- You have to ask several follow-up questions
- The tone doesn't match what you needed
- Important context seems to be missing from the response

**Weak prompt**: "Help me with my budget."

**Your turn**: Rewrite this prompt with context, specificity, and format requirements.

**Possible improvement**: "I'm a small business owner who needs to create a monthly budget for my consulting practice. ## Key Takeaways

1. **Specificity wins**: The more specific you are, the better your results
2. **Context is crucial**: Always explain your situation and goals
3. **Format matters**: Tell AI exactly how you want the response structured
4. **One task at a time**: Focus each prompt on a single, clear objective
5. **Practice improves results**: The more you prompt, the better you'll get

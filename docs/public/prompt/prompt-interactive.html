Choose Your Reviewer Type

What kind of restaurant reviewer do you want to be? This sets the foundation for your entire instruction.

🍕 Casual Foodie
Relatable, everyday dining experiences. Perfect for neighborhood spots and comfort food.
⭐ Professional Critic
Authoritative, detailed analysis. High-end establishments and culinary expertise.
🏘️ Local Community Guide
Helpful neighbor sharing honest recommendations. Focus on value and local favorites.
🥂 Fine Dining Expert
Sophisticated palate, luxury experiences. Michelin-worthy establishments and wine pairings.

Pick Your Tone & Style

How do you want to sound when writing reviews? Your tone shapes how readers connect with your content.

😊 Friendly & Conversational
Like chatting with a friend over coffee. Warm, approachable, and easy to read.
📝 Authoritative & Detailed
Comprehensive analysis with culinary knowledge. Professional but accessible.
🎭 Entertaining & Quirky
Fun, memorable writing with personality. Makes dining reviews engaging stories.
💯 Honest & Straightforward
No-nonsense, direct feedback. Tell it like it is with helpful specifics.

What Should You Focus On?

Different diners care about different things. What's most important for your reviews to cover?

🍽️ Food Quality & Taste
Deep dives into flavors, ingredients, preparation, and culinary execution.
🏟️ Service & Atmosphere
The complete dining experience - ambiance, staff, and overall vibe.
💰 Value & Practical Info
Bang for your buck, portions, location, parking, and practical dining details.
📸 Instagram & Experience
Visual appeal, shareability, and memorable moments worth posting about.

Choose Your Review Format

How should your reviews be structured? The format guides readers through your thoughts.

📖 Narrative Story
Tell the dining experience as a story from arrival to departure.
📋 Structured Sections
Clear sections: Food, Service, Atmosphere, Value with ratings or scores.
⚡ Quick Highlights
Scannable format with key points, pros/cons, and bottom-line recommendation.
🔍 Detailed Analysis
Comprehensive breakdown with specific examples and culinary insights.

## Overview of Intro to Prompt Engineering
Generative AI is transforming how businesses **access knowledge, automate tasks, and enhance decision-making.** However, unlocking the full potential of AI **requires more than just asking questions** **prompt engineering.**  

**Prompt engineering is the practice of designing and refining prompts to get precise, relevant, and structured AI responses.** Whether you're crafting simple queries or optimizing AI for complex workflows, understanding how to **control AI behavior, manage context, and leverage knowledge retrieval** will help you get the most value from Sairis.  

This course will **teach you the fundamentals of prompt engineering**, covering key concepts such as **structuring prompts effectively, managing AI instructions, and using RAG-powered knowledge retrieval.**  


## What You'll Learn  
By the end of this course, you will be able to:  

**Craft clear and structured AI prompts** to get accurate, useful responses.  
**Use metaprompts (instructions) to guide AI behavior and maintain consistency.**  
**Understand the difference between AI context and RAG-powered knowledge retrieval.**  
**Optimize AI interactions by structuring prompts, selecting relevant knowledge sources, and refining AI responses over time.**  


## Course Overview  

This **Introduction to Prompt Engineering** course consists of **eight structured lessons**, each focusing on a fundamental skill needed to work effectively with AI in Sairis.  

### **1. Sairis Introduction to Prompt Engineering (101)**  
- Learn the **role of prompts in AI interactions** and how they shape responses.  
- Understand the **basics of AI behavior, context, and response generation.**  

### **2. Writing Clear User Input Prompts (102)**  
- Master the **principles of effective AI communication.**  
- Avoid **vague prompts** and structure input to get **precise, useful AI responses.**  

### **3. Crafting Effective Instructions (Metaprompts) (103)**  
- Define **metaprompts (instructions) to shape AI behavior across interactions.**  
- Standardize **response style, tone, and structure for consistency.**  

### **4. Prompt Design Patterns (104)**  
- Explore different **types of prompts**, including **direct prompts, few-shot prompts, chain-of-thought reasoning, and role-based prompting.**  
- Learn when to use **specific vs. open-ended** prompts for better results.  

### **5. Testing and Refining Your Prompts (105)**  
- Implement a **structured process for testing and improving AI prompts.**  
- Measure prompt effectiveness using **accuracy, consistency, and relevance.**  

### **6. Context vs. RAG: How They Work Together (106)**  
- Understand the **difference between conversation context and AI knowledge retrieval (RAG).**  
- Learn **when to rely on AI memory vs. when to retrieve external knowledge.**  

### **7. Managing Context in Prompts (107)**  
- Learn how **AI processes context in prompts and chat history.**  
- Apply **structured methods to ensure AI retains relevant details in multi-turn conversations.**  

### **8. Working with Knowledge (RAG & Information Domains in Sairis) (108)**  
- Master **RAG-powered AI search** by selecting **which knowledge bases and files AI can retrieve information from.**  
- Learn how to **optimize AI knowledge retrieval for precise, business-specific answers.**

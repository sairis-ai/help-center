# Control Panel User Guide

## What is the Control Panel?

The Control Panel is where you go to tune how the AI works so you can change it exactly to your needs. You can view your chat history, choose which documents the AI uses, change AI models, turn citations on/off, enable live web search, and adjust other settings to get the responses you want.

## How to Open the Control Panel

The Control Panel should already be open on the right side of your screen. If you don't see it try this:

1. Look for the **Control Panel opener/closer icon** in your AI chat
2. Click it to show the panel
3. You'll see three tabs at the top: **Chat**, **Content**, and **Settings**

## The Three Main Sections

![Image](/images/control-panel-2.png)

**Chat** - Your conversation history and saved templates
**Content** - Choose what information the AI can access  
**Settings** - Change how the AI behaves and responds

## Chat: Managing Your Chats

![Image](/images/control-panel-4.png)

### What You Can Do:
- **View Chat History** - See all your previous conversations
- **Use Blueprints** - Apply saved AI templates for different tasks
- **Find Old Chats** - Search through your conversation history

### Quick Actions:
- Click any chat to continue that conversation
- Use search to find specific topics
- Try different blueprints

## Content: Choosing Content

![Image](/images/control-panel-5.png)

### Selecting What the AI Can Use:

**Knowledge Bases** 
- This is where you see and select your Knowledge Bases

**Assigned Content** 
- Check boxes next to individual content
- Good for questions about specific content

**My Content** 
- Upload your own files
- Check boxes next to files you want to use

### Using Your Selected Content:

1. **Pick your sources** - Select knowledge bases ,specific content, or file
2. **See what you chose** - Blue boxes = knowledge bases, White boxes = files  
3. **Ask questions** - The AI will only use your selected sources

**Quick tip:** Remove items by clicking the checkmark, or click "Clear" to remove everything.

## Settings: Customizing Your AI

![Image](/images/control-panel-6.png)

### AI Behavior:
- **AI Model** - Choose which AI version to use
- **Blueprint** - Set how the AI should act 
- **Instructions** - Add custom instructions (if allowed)
- **AI Mode** - Change response style

### Response Options:
- **Citations On/Off** - Show where answers come from
- **Live Web On/Off** - Let AI search the internet
- **Content Filtering** - Block certain types of content

### Quick Actions:
- Turn on citations to see sources
- Enable web search for current information  
- Try different AI modes for different tasks

### Tip:

- Try diffrent settings and play around with it to find your best settings

## Quick Troubleshooting

**Can't see the Control Panel?**
→ Click the opener/closer icon in your chat

**Settings are grayed out?**  → You might not have permission

**AI not using your selected content?**
→ Make sure you clicked "New Content Chat"


**Changes not working?**
→ Some settings need a new chat to take effect

# Control Panel User Guide

## What is the Control Panel?

The Control Panel puts you in the driver's seat of your AI experience. Whether you want to choose specific documents for the AI to reference, change how it responds, or adjust advanced settings, you can do it all right from this panel without interrupting your workflow. Ready to see what's possible? Let's explore.

## How to Open the Control Panel

The Control Panel should already be open on the right side of your screen. If you don't see it, try this:

1. Look for the **Control Panel opener/closer icon** in your AI chat ![Image](/images/control-panel-14.png)
2. Click it to show the panel
3. You'll see three tabs at the top: **Chat**, **Content**, and **Settings**

![Image](/images/control-panel-3.png)

![Image](/images/control-panel-2.png)

## Content: Choosing Content

![Image](/images/control-panel-5.png)

## Knowledge Base Selection

- This is where you see and select your Knowledge Bases

![Image](/images/content-panel-7.png)
1. Click the "Knowledge Bases" accordion panel to expand it
2. View the list of available knowledge bases
3. Each knowledge base appears with:
   - Name 
   - Checkbox for selection
4. Use the search box at the top to filter knowledge bases by name or description

### Selecting Knowledge Bases
To select knowledge bases for your conversation:

1. Click the checkbox next to each knowledge base you want to include
2. Selected knowledge bases appear in the Selected Content area at the top
3. Selecting a knowledge base includes all its content in the conversation context
4. You can combine multiple knowledge bases for broader coverage

## Assigned Content

![Image](/images/control-panel-12.png)

### Search Mode:

This is the default mode. It searches the AI database for your file and only pulls back data related to your conversation. This is useful for large files when you only need to find specific parts based on your questions. Uses fewer tokens for search results.

### Attach Mode:

Makes the entire file content accessible to the AI. This attaches the whole file so you can reference any part of it. Uses more tokens to process the complete document with each request.

---

### Attaching Files

1. Click the "Assigned Content" accordion panel to expand it
2. Choose either search or Attach Mode
3. View the list of available content items across all knowledge bases
4. Each content item appears with:
   - Name/title
5. Use the search box to find specific content by name, type, or topic
6. Click the checkbox next to each content item you want to include
   - This approach is more precise than selecting entire knowledge bases
7. Selected content items appear in the Selected Content area at the top

## Settings: Customizing Your AI

![Image](/images/control-panel-6.png)

### What You Can Change in Settings:

**AI Model** - Switch between different AI versions (like Claude Sonnet 4). Each model has different capabilities and strengths.

**Blueprint** - Choose pre-made templates that change how the AI acts. For example, pick a "Creative Writing" blueprint for storytelling or "Data Analysis" for working with numbers.

**Instructions** - Add your own custom instructions to tell the AI exactly how you want it to behave (only available if you have permission).

**AI Mode** - Change how the AI thinks and responds:

- **Agentic** mode uses more reasoning and problem-solving
- **Q&A** mode focuses more on using your selected content, doesn't reason as much but gives you faster responses

**Citations On/Off** - When turned on, the AI will show you exactly where it got its information from. Helpful for research and fact-checking.

**Live Web On/Off** - Allows the AI to search the internet for current information. Turn this on when you need up-to-date news or recent data.

**Content Filtering** - Controls what types of content the AI can discuss. Helps keep conversations appropriate for your workplace.

## Quick Troubleshooting

**Can't see the Control Panel?**
→ Click the opener/closer icon in your chat

**Settings are grayed out?**  → You might not have permission

**Changes not working?**
→ Some settings need a new chat to take effect

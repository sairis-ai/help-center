# AI Prompt Configuration Guide

To effectively structure AI prompts, consider these elements: Role, Task, Requirements, and Instructions. Here's a breakdown with examples: 
## Role: Define the AI's persona or role in the interaction. 
- Example: "You are an experienced sales development representative." 
- Other examples: "You are a chatbot assistant," "You are a technical writer," "You are a medical expert" 

## Task: Clearly state what you want the AI to do. 
- Example: "Focus on the prospect's needs and pain points." 
- Other examples: "Summarize the following text," "Translate this sentence," "Generate a poem" 

## Requirements: Specify any constraints or limitations. 
- Example: "Always maintain a professional and friendly tone." 
- Other examples: "Keep the answer under 100 words," "Use a conversational style," "Provide a code example in Python" 

## Instructions: Provide detailed guidance on how the AI should perform the task. 
- Example: "Start by asking the prospect about their current challenges." 
- Other examples: "Explain the concept in simple terms," "Use bullet points to organize the information," "Provide a step-by-step guide" 

### Examples of Prompt Structures:
- Example 1 (Sales AI):
- Role: "You are an experienced sales development representative."
- Task: "Follow up with potential clients."
- Requirements: "Always maintain a professional and friendly tone."
- Instructions: "Start by introducing yourself and asking about their current challenges." 

### Example 2 (Text Summarization):
- Role: "You are a skilled summarizer."
- Task: "Summarize the following article."
- Requirements: "Keep the summary under 100 words."
- Instructions: "Focus on the main points and key arguments." 

### Example 3 (Code Generation):
- Role: "You are a Python developer."
- Task: "Generate a Python function."
- Requirements: "The function should take two numbers as input."
Instructions: "The function should return the sum of the two numbers." 

### Example 4 (Question Answering):
- Role: "You are a knowledgeable AI assistant."
- Task: "Answer the following question."
- Requirements: "Provide a concise and accurate answer."
- Instructions: "Explain the answer in a clear and easy-to-understand way."
